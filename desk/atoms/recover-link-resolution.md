# Recover link resolution

## Answer

Use recover link resolution when the need is to inspect what links or transclusions resolve to without producing the fully expanded composed document.

## Supporting points

- Recovery is about resolution rather than final materialization.
- It belongs to the link-oriented behavior surface.
- It should remain conceptually distinct in the refactor.
