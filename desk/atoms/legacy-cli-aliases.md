# Legacy CLI aliases

## Answer

Legacy CLI aliases apply when maintaining continuity for older surfaces that still exist temporarily but are no longer the primary public interface.

## Supporting points

- They preserve migration continuity without defining the preferred surface.
- They should be treated as compatibility behavior rather than as the center of the CLI design.
- Their presence matters mainly during transition.
