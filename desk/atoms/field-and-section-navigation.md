# Field and section navigation

## Answer

Once a store exists, `sections`, `fields`, and `find` are the supported user-facing ways to navigate document structure more deeply than shallow document models allow.

## Supporting points

- The CLI exposes navigation beyond file-level access.
- Section and field surfaces are part of the v1 product shape.
- This is an observable navigation contract, not just an implementation detail.
